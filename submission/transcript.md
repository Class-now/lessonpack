# LessonPack demo video: transcript

The video has no audio. These are the on-screen English captions, with start times.

- **00:00** Meet Aya (fictional): a beginner English learner with an old shared phone and no mobile data.
- **00:05** Before: her teacher's lesson lives online. With no connection, she can't study at home at all.
- **00:11** LessonPack: the teacher pastes a short lesson in plain text: words, a dialogue, questions.
- **00:17** Simple format: 'word = meaning', 'Speaker: line = translation', '+' marks the right answer.
- **00:22** One click (or Enter) makes ONE self-contained HTML file. No account, no server, no upload.
- **00:28** 24 KB. Send it by Bluetooth, USB cable, SD card or a chat app. It never needs the internet again.
- **00:33** After: Aya opens the file with Wi-Fi and data off. Everything works.
- **00:38** Display options: larger text, high contrast, and easy-read spacing for dyslexic readers.
- **00:45** Every word and line has a Listen button. It uses the phone's own offline voice, at normal or slow speed.
- **00:50** If the phone has no speech engine, the buttons are hidden and a note says so. The text is always there.
- **00:55** Translations can be hidden for self-testing. The page is all real headings, labels and buttons for screen readers.
- **01:00** Self-check quiz with immediate feedback, fully keyboard-operable.
- **01:05** Wrong first? 'Not yet, try again' — after two tries it shows a hint from the dialogue.
- **01:10** Typed answers are forgiving about capitals and punctuation.
- **01:15** Progress is saved on the phone itself. Close the file, reopen it tomorrow: it is still there.
- **01:21** Tested: axe-core 0 violations, keyboard-only walkthrough, 320px screens, JavaScript-off and no-speech fallbacks.
- **01:27** LessonPack: one barrier removed. No data, no problem. Open source, MIT. class-now.github.io/lessonpack

All people and lesson content in the video are fictional.
